3Aerodynamics

Shaped for Every Circuit

High Downforce, Low Drag

An evolved aerodynamic package, broader set-up range and updated electronic differential allow the 296 Challenge Evo to adapt as effectively as possible to different track types.

Two reference configurations are available: High Downforce for more technical circuits, and the Low Drag kit, created exclusively for faster ones, so the car can perform at its best in every situation.

4Vehicle Dynamics

Faster, More Stable, More Consistent

Braking Becomes Performance

The car was developed as a single integrated project, with particular attention to the vehicle dynamics controls, the brake pedal force curve and the instrument panel. Updated Traction Control strategies and an upgraded eDiff deliver greater agility and stability.

Braking is just as focused. Carbon-ceramic CCM-R Plus discs work with the ABS Evo Track brake-by-wire system, while increased pedal travel and force give more exacting control over deceleration. Four ABS settings on the Manettino suit dry or wet conditions, prioritizing either maximum performance or stability.

A redesigned instrument panel presents driving information and vehicle dynamics settings more intuitively, with TC and ABS icons that fill to show intervention levels. An optional, easily removable passenger seat lets drivers work with a coach on track.
